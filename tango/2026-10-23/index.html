    How to play

    Tango for October 23, 2026 is a 6×6 board with 6 given cells and 7 = / × signs. Fill every cell with a sun or a moon. Each row and column has as many suns as moons, never three of the same in a row. Cells joined by = match, cells joined by × are different.

    Tap a cell once for a sun, twice for a moon, three times to clear it. Grey cells are given.

    Hint and answer

    Hint: Look for two equal symbols side by side: the cells on both ends of the pair must be the other symbol.
