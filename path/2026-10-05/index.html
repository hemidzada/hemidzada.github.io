How to play

Path for October 5, 2026 is a 6×6 board with 8 numbered dots and 4 walls. Draw one line that passes through every cell, visiting the numbered dots in order from 1. The line cannot cross a wall.

Start on dot 1 and drag. Drag back to undo the last steps, or tap any cell on the line to cut it there.

New to Path? Read the Path rules and strategy guide.

Hint and answer

Hint: Dot 1 sits in row 4, column 1. Fill corners and edges early: a corner cell can only be entered one way.
