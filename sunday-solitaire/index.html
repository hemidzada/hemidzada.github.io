Sunday Solitaire Collection

Klondike, Spider, FreeCell, TriPeaks and Pyramid solitaire

Sunday Solitaire Collection is five classic solitaire card games in one calm, easy-to-read app. Every deal was played to the end by our solver before it shipped, so there are no hopeless deals: if you are stuck, there is a way. A new daily deal arrives for each game every day.

Five solitaire games

  • Klondike: the classic solitaire, Draw 1 or Draw 3.
  • Spider Solitaire: one, two or four suits.
  • FreeCell: every card in sight; safe cards go home by themselves.
  • TriPeaks: one up or one down, clear the three peaks.
  • Pyramid: remove pairs that add up to 13.

Features

  • Every deal checked by a solver to be winnable
  • A daily deal for each game, the same for everyone, with streaks
  • Big, readable cards, illustrated Kings, Queens and Jacks, optional four-colour deck
  • Drag or tap to move, unlimited undo, hints and auto-finish
  • Light and dark themes, card backs and tables to collect
  • Ads only after a win, never during a game; remove them with a one-time purchase
