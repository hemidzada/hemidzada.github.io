Kinwords

Word solitaire, word sort and a daily word puzzle

Kinwords is a calm word solitaire. Deal the cards, open a category and sort every word into the group where it belongs. Each word is an association waiting for its category: find the links and clear the table. A new Daily 4 puzzle arrives every day.

How to play word solitaire

  • Put a category card into an empty slot at the top.
  • Move that category's words onto it. When 3 to 6 words are in place, the category clears and frees the slot.
  • In the columns, a word goes on a word of the same category, and any card fits an empty column.
  • Tap the deck to turn a card. Every action costs one move, so plan ahead.

Daily 4: the daily word puzzle

Sixteen words on a 4×4 grid hide four groups of four. Some groups look alike, so think about the associations before you tap. Four mistakes allowed.
