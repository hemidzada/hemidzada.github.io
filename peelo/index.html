Peelo

Peel the tapes, reveal the masterpiece

Peelo is a calm logic puzzle. Strips of tape are stacked on top of a famous painting. A tape that is still covered by another one will not come off, so look closely, find the strip on top and peel it. Each peel reveals a little more of the picture until the whole painting is free.

How to play

  • Tap a tape to peel it off the picture.
  • A tape with another tape lying over it will not peel. It lifts and snaps back, with no penalty.
  • Work out the order from the top of the stack down until every tape is gone.
  • Big boards can be zoomed with two fingers.

Features

  • 60 levels, from simple pictures to classic public-domain paintings
  • A weekly masterpiece with more than 100 tapes
  • A gallery of every painting you have revealed
  • Soft peel sounds and calm classical music
  • Plays offline, no internet needed
