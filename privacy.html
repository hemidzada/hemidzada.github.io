Privacy Policy — Train Tracks: Logic Puzzle

Last updated: 24 October 2026 · Android app com.studio.traintracks

Developer: Panda.az Studio
Contact: hello@panda.az

What the game stores

Your level progress, coins, themes, streak and settings are stored on your device. There is no account, no registration and no server operated by us, and we do not receive this data (see "Analytics and crash reports" for what Google Firebase receives).

Android backup. If backup is turned on in your phone's settings, Android copies this game data into your own Google account backup (and to a new phone during device-to-device transfer), so your progress comes back after a reinstall or on a new phone. The backup is managed by Google under your account; we cannot see it. Only the game's progress file is included — no ad or consent data. You can turn backup off in Android settings (Google → Backup).

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob: an interstitial ad after some completed levels (at the “Next level” step), and an optional rewarded ad only when you choose “Segment” in the hint panel. No ads interrupt a puzzle you are solving.

Google AdMob and its partners may collect and process data such as the advertising ID, device and app information, approximate location derived from the IP address, and ad interaction data to deliver, measure and personalise ads and to prevent fraud. Where required (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland) the app asks for your consent with Google's User Messaging Platform before ads are requested. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy → Ad privacy choices.

Google privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy
How Google uses information from apps: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

In-app purchase (“Remove ads”)

You can buy “Remove ads” once. Payment is handled entirely by Google Play under Google's terms; we never see your payment details. The app only asks Google Play whether this purchase is owned (for example after a reinstall) and stores a flag on your device. Google Play keeps the purchase history in your Google account.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state — to load ads and send analytics and crash reports; the puzzles work offline.
  • Advertising ID — used by the Google Mobile Ads SDK.
  • Google Play billing — only for the optional “Remove ads” purchase.

Analytics and crash reports (Google Firebase)

The game uses Google Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics and Remote Config to understand how the game is played, to find and fix crashes, and to adjust game settings (for example difficulty or how often ads appear) without an app update.

  • What is collected: game events (for example a level started or finished, a hint or undo used, an ad shown), crash reports and diagnostics (app version, device model, Android version, the stack trace of a crash), and device or app identifiers (the Firebase app instance ID and, where allowed, the advertising ID). No name, e-mail, contacts, photos or precise location.
  • Who processes it: Google, as our service provider (processor). We use it only to improve and fix the game, not to sell or share it. See Firebase privacy and security.
  • How long: analytics events are kept for up to 14 months; crash reports for up to 90 days.
  • Your choices: clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app resets the app instance ID. You can reset or delete the advertising ID in Android settings. To ask us to delete analytics data linked to your device, e-mail hello@panda.az.
  • Data is sent over an encrypted connection (HTTPS).

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13.

Deleting your data

Game data lives on your device and, if enabled, in your Google account's Android backup: uninstall the app or clear its storage in Android settings, and delete the app's backup in your Google account (Google Drive → Backups) if you want that copy gone too. For data processed by Google for advertising, see Google's privacy policy and your device's advertising settings (you can reset or delete the advertising ID there).

Changes

We will update this page if the app's data practices change.
