Sun & Moon puzzle: rules and strategy

Sun & Moon is a binary logic puzzle with suns and moons. Fill the grid so that every row and column holds as many suns as moons, never three of a kind in a line, and every = or × sign is respected.

Sun & Moon rules

  • Every cell holds a sun or a moon.
  • Each row and each column has the same number of suns and moons.
  • No more than two of the same symbol may sit next to each other in a row or column.
  • Cells joined by = are the same. Cells joined by × are opposite.

How to solve Sun & Moon: 5 tips

1. Break every pair

Two suns side by side must have a moon on each open end. The same goes for two moons.

2. Fill the gap

A sun, an empty cell and a sun in a line force a moon into the gap, because three suns would touch.

3. Count to finish a line

When a row already has all the suns it can hold, every empty cell left in it is a moon.

4. Follow the signs

An = or × sign links two cells. As soon as one is known, the other is too, and the result often starts a chain.

5. Use the edges

Signs and pairs near the edge of the board leave fewer options, so they are good places to start.

Sun & Moon puzzle FAQ

How do you play Sun & Moon?

Fill the grid with suns and moons so every row and column is balanced, no three of a kind touch in a line, and the = and × signs between cells are respected.

Is there a daily Sun & Moon puzzle app?

Yes. KinQueens for Android has a new Sun & Moon board every day, the same for every player, plus levels from easy to expert.

What do = and × mean in Sun & Moon?

= means the two cells hold the same symbol. × means they hold opposite symbols.
