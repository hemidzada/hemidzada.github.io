Privacy Policy — Escape Getaway

Last updated: 31 October 2026 · Android app com.pandaaz.getaway

Developer: Panda.az Studio
Contact: hello@panda.az

Escape Getaway: Police Chase is an arcade driving game. We do not collect, share or sell personal information ourselves. Ads are served by Google AdMob as described below.

What the game stores

Your coins, best score, unlocked cars, selected car and the sound setting are saved on your device. No account or registration is needed, and the game itself works without an internet connection.

Ads

  • The game shows a full-screen ad now and then after a run, and an optional video you can choose to watch to double the coins of a run.
  • Ads come from Google AdMob. To show and count ads, AdMob receives technical data such as the device model, operating system, app version, the advertising ID and an approximate location from the IP address. See how Google uses data from apps that use its services.
  • Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA and the UK), the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.
  • Watching an optional video can also give 30 minutes without full-screen ads.

Analytics and crash reports (Google Firebase)

The game uses Google Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics and Remote Config to understand how the game is played, to find and fix crashes, and to adjust game settings (for example how often ads appear) without an app update.

  • What is collected: game events (for example a run started or ended and how long it lasted, an ad shown, the 30-minute no-ads offer used), crash reports and diagnostics (app version, device model, Android version, the stack trace of a crash), and device or app identifiers (the Firebase app instance ID and, where allowed, the advertising ID). No name, e-mail, contacts, photos or precise location.
  • Who processes it: Google, as our service provider (processor). We use it only to improve and fix the game, not to sell or share it. See Firebase privacy and security.
  • How long: analytics events are kept for up to 14 months; crash reports for up to 90 days.
  • Your choices: clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app resets the app instance ID. You can reset or delete the advertising ID in Android settings. To ask us to delete analytics data linked to your device, e-mail hello@panda.az.
  • Data is sent over an encrypted connection (HTTPS).

Ratings

The game may show Google Play's own rating window after a good run. Google handles it; we do not see who rated or what they wrote unless they publish a public review.

Children

Escape Getaway is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
