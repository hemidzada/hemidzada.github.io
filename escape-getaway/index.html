Escape Getaway: Police Chase

Outrun the cops in a wild 3D car chase

Escape Getaway is an arcade police chase game. Steer left and right, dodge traffic, drift around corners and make the cops crash into each other. Every crash raises your wanted level and the chase gets wilder: roadblocks, cops cutting you off and a police helicopter with a searchlight.

How to play

  • Tap left or right to steer. Speed builds on straight asphalt; dirt roads and sharp turns slow you down.
  • Break line of sight to lose the cops, or make them crash into traffic and each other.
  • Car on fire? Jump out and grab any slow car on the road, even a police car for bonus points.
  • Pick up nitro, shields, monster wheels, bombs and repair kits on the way.

Features

  • Endless police chase with cruisers, SUVs, roadblocks and a helicopter
  • 14 cars to unlock with coins, each with its own speed, handling and armor
  • Highways, bridges, canals, snow and desert districts, day and night, rain and snow
  • Things to smash: lamp posts, trees, gas pumps and billboards (cartoon crashes, no blood)
  • Plays offline, no internet needed
