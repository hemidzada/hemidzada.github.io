How to play Queens, Zip and Tango

  • Queens: place one crown in every row, every column and every colour region. Crowns may never touch, not even at a corner.
  • Zip: draw one line that fills every cell, passing the numbers in order. Walls block the way.
  • Tango: fill the grid with suns and moons. Each row and column holds as many suns as moons, never more than two of a kind side by side.

Full rules and solving tips: Queens puzzle guide, Zip puzzle guide, Tango puzzle guide.

Features

  • Every puzzle has exactly one solution, checked by a solver
  • Graded difficulty, rated by the logic each level needs
  • Daily puzzles with a streak and bigger boards at the weekend
  • Best times, undo and smart hints
  • Plays offline, progress kept by Android backup
  • Light and dark mode with large, clear boards

Made for anyone who enjoys a five-minute brain workout or an evening of expert logic.
