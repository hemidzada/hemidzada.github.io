Kindoku: Cat Sudoku Puzzle

One cat per row, column and cushion

Kindoku is a calm logic puzzle with cats. The board is split into colored cushions. Place one cat in every row, every column and every cushion, and no two cats may touch, not even at the corners. Every board has exactly one solution, so you never need to guess.

Coming soon to Google Play Privacy policy

How to play

  • Tap a square once to mark it with an X, tap again to place a cat.
  • Each row, each column and each colored cushion gets exactly one cat.
  • Cats need their own space: no two cats may touch, not even diagonally.
  • Stuck? Use a hint. Every puzzle can be solved by logic alone.

Features

  • Five looks: cozy cats, garden animals, classic crowns, dark knights and a castle kingdom
  • A new daily puzzle every day
  • Every board has one solution, made for pure logic
  • Hints when you need them
  • Plays offline, no internet needed
