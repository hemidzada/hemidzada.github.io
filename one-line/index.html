Draw One Line Puzzle Game

Draw each figure in one stroke

Draw One Line is a calm brain puzzle: trace every line of a figure in one stroke, without lifting your finger and without drawing any line twice. Each puzzle is a real picture, not a grid: a heart, a fish, a crown, a lighthouse, a castle. Close the last line and the whole figure lights up along the path you drew.

How to play

  • Put your finger on a point and drag along the lines.
  • Every line must be drawn once. A double line is drawn twice.
  • Took a wrong turn? Drag back along your line to undo it. No timer, no lives.
  • Stuck? A hint shows the next line as a running light.

Features

  • Puzzles drawn as real pictures, from simple shapes to castles
  • New rules as you go: portals, locks and keys, and figures that must end on the star
  • A glowing line on a night sky, with a soft note for every line you draw
  • Plays offline, no sign-in
  • Remove ads with a single purchase
