Privacy Policy — Panda.az runner game

Last updated: 14 November 2026 · Android app com.pandaaz.stickrunner

Developer: Panda.az Studio
Contact: hello@panda.az

This is a rooftop runner game. It does not collect, share or sell any personal information. It asks for no permissions, needs no account and makes no network connections.

What the game stores

Your best times and settings are saved on your device only. Nothing is sent to us or to anyone else.

If Android backup is on for your Google account, Android may include this data in your device backup. That backup is handled by Google; we cannot see it.

Ads, purchases, analytics

This version has no ads, no in-app purchases, no analytics and no crash reporting. If a later version adds any of these, this page will say so, with the new date, before that version is released.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
