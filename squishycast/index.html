SquishyCast

Squishy toy ASMR game for kids

Open a mystery box and find a squishy dumpling inside. SquishyCast is a cozy toy game for kids: unbox kawaii squishies, squeeze and stretch them with satisfying ASMR sounds, feed them, dress them up and collect them all.

How to play

  • Open a mystery box: peel, lift, twist and tear it open.
  • Squeeze, pull and poke your squishy. It giggles, wobbles and reacts.
  • Feed it snacks, pop bubbles and make it bounce.
  • Add funny eyes, blush, glasses and stickers anywhere on your toy.

Features

  • 88 squishies to collect: dumplings, fruit, boba tea, ice cream, animals and more
  • Common, rare, epic and secret toys
  • Calm, satisfying ASMR sounds: slow-rise foam, sticky mochi, jiggly jelly
  • Earn coins by playing to open new boxes
  • Box odds are shown before you open
  • No chat and no account needed, works offline

Made for kids who love squishy toys, fidget toys, slime, blind boxes and unboxing videos.
