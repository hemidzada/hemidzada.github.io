Privacy Policy — Kaboball

Last updated: 27 November 2026 · Android app com.pandaaz.ballrun

Developer: Panda.az Studio
Contact: hello@panda.az

Kaboball is an arcade ball arena game. We do not collect, share or sell personal information ourselves. Ads are served by Google AdMob as described below.

What the game stores

Your progress, coins, balls and settings are saved on your device. No account or registration is needed. The game works without an internet connection.

If Android backup is on for your Google account, Android may include this progress in your device backup so a new phone or a reinstall keeps it. That backup is handled by Google; we cannot see it.

Ads

  • The game shows ads from Google Mobile Ads (AdMob), for example a full-screen ad now and then between runs, or a rewarded ad you choose to watch for a bonus.
  • Ads come from Google AdMob. To show and count ads, AdMob receives technical data such as the device model, operating system, app version, the advertising ID and an approximate location from the IP address. See how Google uses data from apps that use its services.
  • Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA and the UK), the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, using Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP). You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

Children

Kaboball is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
